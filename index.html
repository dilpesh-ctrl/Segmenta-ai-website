The problem

Bid floors are the last big lever nobody is pulling properly.

In-app bidding was supposed to make floors irrelevant. It didn't. Bid shading is everywhere, and most networks treat your floor as a signal about what the impression is worth. Set it too low and you leave money on every high-value impression. Set it too high and you lose fill on the users who were never going to pay much anyway.

The right answer is different for every player. The problem is that finding it has always meant putting somebody else's code in your game.

One floor for everyone

Your whale and your never-payer see the same floor, in the same country group, at the same price.

Optimisation you can't ship

The vendor with the answer needs an SDK, a QA cycle and a store submission. By the time it's live, the quarter's gone.

Insight you can't act on

Your mediation dashboard tells you your average eCPM. It doesn't tell you which players generate it.

Why no SDK

"No SDK" isn't a limitation. It's the product.

Live in a day, not a quarter

No integration ticket. No engineer pulled off the roadmap. No QA pass. No app store review. No waiting for a new build to reach enough users before your optimisation learns anything.

Zero risk to the thing you actually care about

Third-party SDKs are a leading cause of ANRs and cold-start regressions in mobile games. A monetisation gain that costs you a third of a point on your crash-free rate isn't a gain. Segmenta adds no bytes, no threads and no network calls to your app.

We optimise your history, not just your future

Because we read your mediation data rather than generate it, Segmenta can analyse impressions that already happened. You get your first bucket analysis from data you already own — before you've agreed to change anything.

We never touch the auction

Segmenta isn't in your waterfall, isn't a demand partner and doesn't take a position in your ad calls. Your mediation setup stays your mediation setup. We change the numbers in it, not the plumbing under it.

Questions

The things ad ops teams ask first.

Do I really not have to integrate anything?

Correct. Segmenta reads your mediator's user-level ad revenue API and writes back through its ad unit management API. Both are server-to-server. Nothing changes in your app binary.

How can you optimise per user without being in the app?

We don't optimise per user in real time — we optimise per value bucket, and route buckets to ad units whose floors we control. For floor pricing this captures most of the available uplift, because the underlying signal (what a player is worth to advertisers) is stable across sessions. Decisions that genuinely depend on in-session state, like interstitial cooldowns, need an SDK, and we're honest that we don't do those.

Which mediators do you support?

Any mediator that exposes user-level ad revenue data and programmatic ad unit management. If you're not sure whether yours qualifies, ask us — the answer takes about a minute.

What if it doesn't work?

The A/B test tells you, in your own dashboard, in your own numbers. Then you revoke the API key. There is no removal project.

Do you take a cut of my ad revenue as a demand partner?

No. We are not in your auction and we don't sell your inventory.

How much data do you need before recommendations are useful?

Roughly 30 days of impression-level history, or 14 days at higher volumes. Where historic data is available through the API, we can often produce a first analysis immediately.
